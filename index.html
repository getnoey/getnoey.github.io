What Noey does

Gets through the queue your data team never clears.

Every data team has a backlog of small requests, half-built pipelines and dashboards nobody has time for. Noey takes those on, and shows its work at every step.

Answers ad-hoc requests

Ask in plain English. Noey finds the right tables, writes the SQL, runs it and explains how it got the answer.

Builds pipelines

Describe the table you need. Noey designs the transformation, tests it against your data and hands it to you to approve.

Builds charts and dashboards

Turns a question into a chart, and a set of charts into a dashboard your team can come back to.

Learns your data

Builds a knowledge base of your servers, tables, metrics and business terms. Give it your data dictionary and it starts ahead.

How it works

Set it up like a new hire, not a new tool.

Noey gets better the more it knows about your data, and you stay in charge of what it knows and what it does.

01

Connect

Add your PostgreSQL, MySQL and SQL Server servers, as many as you run. You choose which databases Noey can see.

02

Teach

Noey scans your schemas and reads your data dictionary into a knowledge base. You review what it learned before it uses it.

03

Delegate

Ask in chat. Noey plans the work, runs it across servers, checks the result and stops for your approval before changing anything.

Built for sensitive data

Your data team stays in control.

A tool that touches production data has to earn trust. These are the rules Noey is built around.

Nothing changes without approval

Every change to your data is shown to you first and runs only after you approve it. If approval can't be reached, the change doesn't run.

It checks its own work

Noey validates results before it reports them, shows the queries it ran, and tells you when it isn't sure.

Your data stays where it is

Noey reads your data to answer you. Your data stays stored in your own systems; Noey's servers pass it to the model and don't keep it.

Your model, your key

Use OpenAI, Anthropic or Gemini models with your own API key.
